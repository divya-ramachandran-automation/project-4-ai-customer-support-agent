ShopEase Customer Support FAQ

1. Order Tracking
Customers can track an order using the tracking link sent to their registered email address after the order ships. If the tracking link is unavailable, contact support with the order number. Tracking details may take up to 24 hours to appear after shipment.

2. Delivery Time
Standard delivery usually takes 3–5 business days after shipment. Delivery to remote locations may take longer. Public holidays and unexpected courier delays can affect delivery estimates.

3. Returns
Eligible unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Products must be in their original condition and packaging. Some categories may have additional return restrictions. Customers should contact support to confirm eligibility before sending an item back.

4. Refunds
After an approved return is received and inspected, refunds are generally processed within 5–7 business days. The time taken for the refunded amount to appear depends on the bank or payment provider.

5. Cancellation
Orders can generally be cancelled before they are shipped. Once an order has shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible. Customers should contact support as soon as possible to request cancellation.

6. Payment Methods
ShopEase accepts credit cards, debit cards, and supported UPI payments. Payment availability may vary depending on the customer's location and payment provider.

7. Damaged or Incorrect Items
Customers who receive a damaged or incorrect item should contact support within 48 hours of delivery. They should provide the order number and photographs of the item and packaging.

8. Contact Support
Customers can contact ShopEase support through the Help section of the website. They should include their order number when asking about a specific order.

9. Missing Information
If the FAQ does not contain enough information to answer a question, the support agent should not invent a policy. It should explain that the information is unavailable and recommend contacting the support team.
